# Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within thirty days of purchase when the order is unused and the account is in good standing.

Refund requests must include an order identifier, purchase date, and reason code. Agents should not approve refunds without verifying order status.

Escalate cases involving fraud signals, duplicate refund attempts, or missing order history.
